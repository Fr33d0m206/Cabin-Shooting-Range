# Cabin Shooting Range

**Version 0.2.4** | **Road to Vostok** | **Metro Mod Loader**

[Download the latest release](https://github.com/Fr33d0m206/Cabin-Shooting-Range/releases/latest)

Requires Metro Mod Loader. Cheat Menu is optional and provides the F6 Furniture Spawner. See [installation instructions](INSTALL.md) before starting.

### Build your range. Set up your own kill house. See how good your aim really is.

I wanted something more fun than shooting at random objects outside the cabin, so this adds a proper set of practice targets you can arrange however you want.

Start with a few targets out back, build a longer shooting lane, or put together a whole practice area with walls, doorways and windows. Move things around, save different setups, and run timed rounds to beat your high score.

## 🎯 Twelve Pieces to Build With

| Piece | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Paper Bullseye** | Traditional scoring rings for precision practice. |
| **Paper Silhouette** | Human-shaped target with separate head, center mass and body scoring. |
| **Steel Torso** | Hanging human-shaped steel plate with reactive movement and headshot scoring. |
| **Round Gong** | Classic circular steel target suspended from chains. |
| **Square Plate** | Hanging steel plate for practicing controlled, accurate shots. |
| **Diamond Plate** | A different profile for mixing up your shooting lanes. |
| **Five-Plate Rack** | Five independently reacting paddles that fall when hit. |
| **Dueling Tree** | Six flipping paddles for moving between targets. |
| **No-Shoot Silhouette** | A penalty target to make you think before pulling the trigger. |
| **Practice Wall** | Modular wall section for building rooms and shooting lanes. |
| **Practice Doorway** | Open doorway for connecting rooms and setting up angles. |
| **Practice Window** | Window opening for practicing shots through cover. |

## 🔩 Worn Surfaces and Reactive Steel

These targets are built to look like equipment that has actually been used.

- Chipped paint, rust, scratches and weathered surfaces.
- Worn paper backers and timber frames.
- Beveled steel plates with visible mounting hardware.
- Connected hanging chains that move with their plates.
- Visible bullet impacts on target faces.
- Steel targets that swing, fall or flip when hit.

**Falling and flipping paddles automatically return after 3 seconds.** Each paddle resets independently, so you can keep practicing without walking over to stand everything back up.

## 🏆 Scoring That Makes Practice More Fun

Accurate shots earn more points. Different target shapes use scoring that fits their design.

| Hit | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| **Bullseye center** | 100 |
| **Other bullseye rings** | 90 down to 10 |
| **Silhouette or torso headshot** | 100 |
| **Center mass** | 100 |
| **Body hit** | 50 |
| **Other steel plate center** | 100 |
| **Other steel plate edge** | 50 |
| **Paper outside the silhouette** | 10 |
| **No-shoot target** | -50 |

**Headshots only count on human-shaped targets.** Circular plates, gongs and other geometric targets use center and edge scoring.

You will occasionally get **HEADSHOT**, **BULLSEYE**, **EPIC!**, **ON FIRE!** and points popups for a little encouragement. They are spaced out so you can still see what you are shooting at.

## ⏱️ Timed Challenges and High Scores

Choose a **30, 60, 90 or 120 second round** and see how many points you can earn.

- Practice freely or start a timed challenge.
- Track your score, hits and headshots.
- Keep separate records for different maps, arrangements and round lengths.
- Get a **NEW HIGH SCORE!** announcement when you finish a round that beats your saved record.
- Reset the targets for fresh faces and another attempt.

Moving targets or changing the layout ends the current challenge, keeping records tied to the setup you actually practiced on.

## 🪑 Works Like Furniture

All twelve pieces are available through **F6 → Furniture** in the existing Cheat Menu. Look for items beginning with **Range:** and add them to your furniture catalog.

For normal purchasing, the pieces also join the **Generalist trader's stock pool**, with values ranging from **25 to 200**. Availability follows the trader's usual stock and resupply.

Once a piece is in your catalog, use the normal furniture controls to place, move and rotate it.

## 🛠️ Build Your Own Practice Area

Press **F4 outdoors** to open the range editor. **Alt+R** is also available as an alternative.

- Choose a target or room piece and place it on clear ground.
- Click and hold an existing piece to drag it.
- Rotate pieces to create different shooting angles.
- Save up to **three scenarios per map**.
- Build arrangements with up to **40 pieces**.
- Keep your committed outdoor layouts between visits.

On your first visit to **Village**, six starter targets are placed around the outside of the cabin. You can rearrange them or build your own setup from scratch.

Put a no-shoot target beside a silhouette, scatter steel plates between windows, or build a little kill house and practice moving through it. That is the whole idea. Make something fun to practice on.

## Getting Started

1. Enable **Cabin Shooting Range** in Metro Mod Loader and restart the game.
2. Head outside the cabin into **Village**.
3. Try the starter targets or press **F4** to arrange your range.
4. Open **F6 → Furniture** to add more **Range:** pieces.
5. Start a timed round and give yourself a score to beat.
